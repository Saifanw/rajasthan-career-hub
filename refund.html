Refund, Cancellation and Delivery Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026.

What you are buying

A digital product: the paid details of one job (HR or hiring contact, company career page link, hiring tips, whichever are listed on that job page). The price is shown before you pay. Nothing is shipped. Prices are in INR.

Delivery

The details appear on the same job page right after your payment is confirmed, usually within seconds. Your Payment ID is shown on screen. You can open the details again on any device by entering that Payment ID and the email you used at checkout. If you paid but cannot see the details, contact us and we will fix it.

When you get a refund

  • The contact or career link shown is wrong or does not work, and we cannot give you a correct one within 48 hours of your request.
  • The job page promised details that were not shown after payment.
  • You were charged twice for the same job.
  • The employer asked us to remove the details before you could use them.

Please ask within 7 days of the payment date.

Not refundable

Change of mind after the details were shown, the HR not replying or not hiring you (we cannot control that), and misuse of the details or the site.

How to ask

Message us at saif.anwar.jobs@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 63505 66010 with your Payment ID and the email you paid with. Tell us briefly what went wrong.

When you get the money

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 working days. Your bank may take extra time to show it. After a refund, access to those job details stops.

Cancellation

There is no subscription or auto-renewal. You pay once for one job, so there is nothing to cancel later.
