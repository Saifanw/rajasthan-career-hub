Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026.

What we collect

  • When you pay: your email, your mobile number if you enter it, the job you unlocked, the amount and the Razorpay payment reference.
  • When you contact us or send an inquiry: the details you write in the form or send on WhatsApp or email.
  • When you browse: basic, anonymous visit information such as pages viewed and clicks, used to improve the site.
  • On your device: we save a small note (your email and Payment ID) in your browser so your unlocked details open again on that device.

Why we use it

To deliver the job details you paid for, to prove and support a payment or refund, to answer your messages, and to keep the site working and safe. We do not sell your personal data.

Who handles it

Payments are processed by Razorpay; we never see or store your card or bank details. Site data is stored with Google Firebase and Supabase, and the website is hosted on GitHub Pages. These providers process data for us under their own terms.

HR and employer contact details

The HR or hiring contacts shown on paid job pages are provided by the employer or taken from the employer's own public job posts or career pages. They are shown only to people who pay for that job. If you are the person named and want your details corrected or removed, write to saif.anwar.jobs@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 63505 66010. We act within 48 hours.

How long we keep it

Payment records are kept as long as needed for accounts, tax and dispute handling. Other data is kept only as long as it is useful and then deleted.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, subject to records we must keep by law. You can clear the saved note on your device by clearing your browser site data.

Children
